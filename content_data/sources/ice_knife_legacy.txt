Ice Knife legacy rules reference, reviewed 2026-09-20.
Official reference: https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2384-ice-knife
Rules baseline: 2014. This spell is not in SRD 5.1.

Implementation summary: first-level conjuration, one action, range 60 feet,
somatic and material components, instantaneous. Select one creature and make
a ranged spell attack. A hit inflicts 1d10 piercing damage. After either attack
outcome, a five-foot cold burst includes the selected target and nearby
creatures. Each makes a Dexterity save; failure inflicts 2d6 cold damage and
success avoids that cold damage. Higher slots add one cold d6 per extra level.
