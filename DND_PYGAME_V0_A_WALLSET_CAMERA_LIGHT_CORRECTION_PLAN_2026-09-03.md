# Pygame V0 A-Wallset, Camera, and Boundary-Light Correction Plan

Status: `REVIEW CANDIDATE — NO IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORIZED`

This document supersedes only the active boundary-asset claims in
`DND_PYGAME_V0_WOODEN_BOUNDARY_VISUAL_CORRECTION_PLAN_2026-09-03.md` and adds
the missing camera/face-light correction. The wooden document and both older
corrective plans remain unchanged as evidence of rejected candidates. The
accepted in-process Event, reduction, queue, Water, flame, picking, and
lifetime design remains governing.

## 1. What is wrong now

The current B-family result is not an acceptable doorway system:

- `Wall B4` is a shortened/broken palisade segment, not an authored closed
  state paired with an authored open state;
- opening the door removes the Sprite completely, so the visual has no stable
  frame and no real open pose;
- the palisade is visually specialized while this first map needs a plain,
  reusable wall; and
- boundary treatment calls `_disclosure()` on both incident cells and uses the
  brightest effective light for the whole Sprite. A torch on the interior can
  therefore illuminate the exterior face when the camera is outside.

The finite direction-to-pose table implemented by the wooden correction is
correct and remains. The error is no longer the table; it is the selected
family, incomplete door composition, missing camera vocabulary, and
non-directional choice of the boundary's subjective light value.

## 3. One cardinal vocabulary

Every diagnostic, helper, and test uses these words:

- **engine direction**: a `CardinalDirection` on the map plane;
- **owner support**: the Tile that owns the boundary object;
- **neighbor support**: the adjacent Tile reached in the engine direction;
- **camera quadrant**: one of Q0 through Q3;
- **camera viewpoint**: the map corner from which the scene is viewed;
- **asset pose**: the E/N/S/W suffix selected after camera rotation; and
- **screen direction**: an arrow generated by projection, never used as an
  engine or asset direction.

The engine's cardinal deltas are fixed by the current world code:

```text
NORTH = (0, +1)    EAST = (+1, 0)
SOUTH = (0, -1)    WEST = (-1, 0)
```

The camera and projection table is:

| Quadrant | Camera viewpoint | Looking toward | Map N on screen | Map E on screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q0 | NE | SW | down-left | down-right |
| Q1 | SE | NW | up-left | down-left |
| Q2 | SW | NE | up-right | up-left |
| Q3 | NW | SE | down-right | up-right |

The already-correct asset-pose table remains:

| Engine direction | Q0 | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NORTH | N | W | S | E |
| EAST | E | N | W | S |
| SOUTH | S | E | N | W |
| WEST | W | S | E | N |

`camera_pose()` stays the single pure direction conversion. No screen-space
name enters world state, and no raster is rotated at runtime.

## 5. Directional boundary-face treatment

The engine continues to own light propagation, optical blocking, darkvision,
magical darkness, and observer-effective light. Pygame does not cast rays or
recompute any of them. It selects which already-reduced incident support
describes the wall face visible from the current camera.

For a boundary with owner position `P`, outward cardinal unit vector `D`, and
camera viewpoint vector `C`:

```text
if dot(C, D) > 0: displayed face support = P + D
else:             displayed face support = P
```

The four viewpoint vectors are the engine-plane diagonals:

```text
Q0 NE = (+1,+1)   Q1 SE = (+1,-1)
Q2 SW = (-1,-1)   Q3 NW = (-1,+1)
```

Because a boundary direction is cardinal, the dot product is never zero.

Geometry disclosure remains incident: a boundary may be drawn when at least
one of its two incident supports is current or remembered, subject to the
existing door-object disclosure rule. Treatment is face-specific:

- if the displayed face support is visible, use that support's existing
  observer-effective `LightLevel`;
- if it is only seen, use the existing memory treatment;
- if it is undisclosed while the reverse face disclosed the structure, use
  the same memory treatment rather than copying light from the hidden side;
- never use `max()` across incident light values; and
- never read objective `WorldTileState.resolved_light` to bypass the selected
  observer's reduction.

For the authored EAST barrier, the torch and observer are west/interior. Q0
and Q1 display its east/exterior faces, while Q2 and Q3 display its
west/interior faces. Closed-door screenshots must therefore never project the
interior torch value onto Q0/Q1 exterior wall faces. Q2/Q3 may use the
engine-provided interior effective light.

This is presentation of a directional surface from existing cell values, not
a new directional-light transport system. If later art supplies normals or
per-face light maps, that is a separate renderer enhancement.

## 6. Straight walls, real door states, and corners

### 6.1 Straight wall

A disclosed stone `DirectionalWall` selects A1 by `camera_pose()` and uses the
owner Tile's existing projected support contact and elevation. Its treatment
comes from section 5.

The proving battlefield's eleven walls return to their honest default
`Material.STONE` and a plain-stone name. Keep the general `DirectionalWall`
material field and builder forwarding already introduced; it is valid engine
data and other authored wooden walls remain possible. Do not add an A-family
subclass or asset field to the engine.

### 6.2 Door

The existing wooden `DirectionalDoor` maps to one stable A9 stone frame plus
one wooden leaf at the same contact:

- closed: A9 frame + Door A1 leaf;
- open: A9 frame + Door A2 leaf; and
- reclosed: A9 frame + Door A1 leaf.

Frame and leaf use the same pose and face treatment. They are two draw
primitives representing one existing door UUID and one existing `is_open`
state. Opening and closing still come only from the current concrete door
actions and their admitted spatial/sensory Events. Do not create frame/leaf
items, events, timers, or animation state, and do not assign `is_open` in the
renderer.

### 6.3 Corner

When exactly two disclosed, same-height, stone `DirectionalWall` facts share
one owner Tile and occupy perpendicular sides, the renderer may represent
them with the corresponding A2 corner pose from section 2. Both original wall
UUIDs remain in frame evidence; neither component is merged, suppressed in
state, or rewritten.

Corner selection is a small pure grouping inside boundary drawing, not a
world index or polymorphic gameplay type. Parallel/opposite pairs, doors,
mixed materials, unequal heights, and more than two walls do not qualify and
remain independent primitives.

An A2 raster contains two visible faces but only one RGB image. If both legs
resolve to the same treatment, draw A2 once. If the face treatments differ,
draw the two corresponding A1 legs independently with their own treatments;
do not average them or leak the brighter side across the corner. This finite
fallback preserves directional-light truth until the asset library supplies
authored face masks.

The V0 battlefield need not change its mechanics merely to manufacture a
corner. A focused render proof constructs the existing two-wall cold states,
exercises all four direction pairs and all four cameras, and visually checks
both the A2 equal-treatment path and the two-leg mixed-treatment path.
