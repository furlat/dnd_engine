# Native composition audit for fixed creature artwork

Date: 2026-09-30  
Scope: read-only study of production native content. This records reusable composition and mechanics for the art-led fixed NPC roster; it does not enumerate art variants or implement/import content.

## Existing content owners

- `dnd/monsters/srd_roster.py` owns SRD-derived creature factories, common stat/materialization policy, weapon possession grants, and the reusable trait wiring. It declares Commoner, Bandit, Cultist, Guard, Tribal Warrior, Kobold, Acolyte, Scout, Thug, Spy, Berserker, Bandit Captain, Priest, Cult Fanatic, Knight, Veteran, Mage, Orc, Hobgoblin, Bugbear, Gnoll, Ogre, Wolf, Dire Wolf, Zombie, Ogre Zombie, Ghoul, and Dretch (source anchors and CR are declared alongside each; see `_SRD_CREATURE_FACTS`, lines 1077–1105). These are useful foundations selected by mechanical fit, not a claim that their whole SRD stat blocks are exact.
- `dnd/monsters/bestiary.py` and `bestiary_content.py` own a separate canonical Goblin/Skeleton line: Goblin, Skeleton, Goblin Archer, Generic Caster, Goblin Caster, Skeleton Warrior, Skeleton Archer and Skeleton Warlock. `bestiary_content.py` registers stable content declarations and composes the corresponding factory; the legacy builders remain in `bestiary.py` (declarations/builders at `bestiary_content.py:300–605`; Goblin equipment at `bestiary.py:180–195`; skeleton gear at `:290–305`; other variants at `:311–425`, `:532–550`, `:676–790`). This path is where the established bespoke goblin/skeleton play identities live.
- `dnd/monsters/demon_variants.py` owns Corrosive Demon and Dread Demon, each composed directly over the SRD Dretch and adding an existing body-response profile (`demon_variants.py:19–53`). This is demonstrated reuse of a small shared native foundation plus distinct reusable condition/effect behavior.
- `dnd/content/characters/builds.py` and `premades.py` own player characters and class progression. This is a richer authored humanoid path for modular characters, not a required class identity for every fixed monster sprite. `CharacterAppearance` carries body category/tint/head/hair/beard facts (`builds.py:50–73`), while the normal build composes origin, classes, gear and appearance. Existing premades are assembled from frozen level/choice records (`premades.py`).

## Implemented mechanics that can be reused

`dnd/monsters/traits.py` has data/action/condition/event-handler implementations wired by `srd_roster.py`. The implementation map (`dnd/monsters/SRD_TRAIT_IMPLEMENTATION_MAP.md:1–73`) identifies: Pack Tactics, Sunlight Sensitivity, Dark Devotion, Brave, keen senses, Cunning Action, Aggressive, Multiattack, Martial Advantage, Sneak Attack, Brute, Surprise Attack, wolf bite/prone, Ghoul claws/paralysis, Undead Fortitude, Parry, Rampage and natural Bite. These are reusable behavior families; each roster entry need not get a unique handler.

The most relevant direct matches are:

- Goblinoid/skirmisher: Nimble Escape (bonus-action Hide/Disengage) is registered by the bespoke Goblin factory (`bestiary.py:68–96`, installed in `create_goblin`); the SRD traits also support Aggressive and Pack Tactics. Existing Goblin Archer is a bespoke shortbow + scimitar + offhand dagger variant, not a universal bow-only recipe (`bestiary.py:311–414`).
- Orc/elite humanoid: SRD Orc includes Aggressive; Hobgoblin Martial Advantage; Bugbear Brute and Surprise Attack; Knight and Bandit Captain Parry; Leadership/Multiattack and other SRD traits are separately represented. Use a fitting equipment recipe (e.g. axe or sword and shield) independently from these behavior blocks.
- Undead: Zombie/Ogre Zombie Undead Fortitude; Ghoul bite/claw paralysis; Skeleton Warrior/Archer/Warlock are existing bespoke compositions. Skeleton Archer's Mark Target is an additional authored ability (`skeleton_abilities.py`, `bestiary_content.py:553–587`), not a default property of all skeleton art.
- Demon: Dretch is the implemented small fiend foundation with bite/claw Multiattack, cold/fire/lightning resistance and poison immunity. Corrosive/Dread body responses are implemented examples. Larger/beastlike demon art does not thereby inherit a supported large-demon stat block.
- Animal: Wolf/Dire Wolf include natural Bite, Pack Tactics, keen hearing/smell and prone rider. Wolf is the immediate GreyWolf-like foundation; proposed pounce, venom, flight or other riders require evidence and, where absent, new mechanics.

Natural attacks are action templates, not fabricated carried weapons. `NaturalAttack` resolves through the ordinary attack outcome pipeline while defining its own die, damage type and range (`traits.py:585–700`); the roster registers natural Bite for Gnoll/Wolf families (`srd_roster.py:875–910`, `:949–985`). Thus visible teeth/claws can coexist with held gear and do not consume hand slots. Conversely, an axe, sword, bow, shield, armor, or staff visible in fixed art should be represented by the closest actual item possession/loadout where supported. Item definitions include common real weapons and armor (`dnd/content/items/authored_item_definitions.py:279+`, `:354–464`); SRD creature possession declarations map gear separately from intrinsic/natural items (`srd_roster.py:1200+`).

`dnd/content/items/item_loadouts.py:1–79` provides reusable starting-equipment tuples, and `dnd/content/items/authored_item_builders.py` builds those item identities. Character `builds.py` composes passive blocks, class grants, and possessions without requiring a creature class for a stat-block NPC. Keep ability scores, health, skills, equipment, actions, conditions and spellcasting as ordinary entity data/composers.

## Implemented foundations, partials, and nearby candidates

The SRD source declarations explicitly mark fidelity `PARTIAL` (`bestiary_content.py:130–162`; SRD descriptors/provenance in `srd_roster.py`). Several specific limitations are already documented in the source. Dretch receives 22 HP under the shared hit-dice policy rather than SRD average 18; its LIGHT claws expose an extra native off-hand bonus attack; Fetid Cloud and telepathy are absent (`srd_roster.py:1105`). Do not quietly describe Dretch as exact. Similar source-ledger trait lists are useful for determining whether a visible effect is actually implemented.

Exact implemented reuse means the named current mechanic and its handlers/actions exist. “Nearby SRD design candidate” means the rules are conceptually close but this audit found no evidenced current mechanic for it. Candidates include demon frightful/poisonous aura, breath/burst attacks, flight, regeneration, shadows/phase traits, and particular zombie variants; these need roster-specific rule study before any implementation claim. A creature's spell list also cannot be inferred from a robe, staff, glowing hand or casting pose. Caster foundations exist (Acolyte/Priest/Cult Fanatic/Mage in SRD roster; Generic Caster, Goblin Caster and Skeleton Warlock in bespoke bestiary), and use existing spell-action registrations. Choose a caster composition only where art and intended play justify it; no class level should be inferred from appearance alone.

Equipment support is strongest for SRD-style medieval arms and armor. Firearm-looking artwork must retain a visible firearm identity. This repository has an `environment.arcane_machine_gun` environmental spell-device builder (`dnd/content/items/environment_item_builders.py:338–362`, registered in `authored_item_builders.py:732–734`); it is not evidence of a carried firearm item, reload/ammunition rules, or a firearm attack profile for NPCs. A firearm NPC therefore needs an explicit fit-for-scope design decision and cannot silently receive a crossbow or ordinary weapon while claiming fidelity to the art.

Elite/recolour treatment is a content choice, not an engine framework requirement. Reuse a base creature's blocks and gear, then tune existing health/armor/ability values or compose a present action/condition if the desired challenge calls for it. Do not assume an “Elite” visual label mandates a new class, unique power, or one-off mechanic. A stronger variant with the same visible kit can be an authored parameterized content identity; new mechanics should be shared only when multiple roster members need the same rule.

## Composition guidance for the parent roster study

For each fixed artwork identity, select the closest SRD/bestiary body and role, then specify actual carried gear or natural attacks, existing behavior blocks, and only the smallest shared additions genuinely needed by multiple variants. Keep the fixed Undead art pack on its own content proposals. The modular `NakedBody2` skeleton and modular humanoid are also valid freely authored paths with broader equipment/cosmetic loadouts; they are not aliases for fixed Skeleton pack identities. Existing appearance/body categories establish visual construction data, while frontend presentation selection can remain keyed by stable content identity and ordinary appearance/equipment facts. Native composition must not take sprite paths, source filenames, frame names or renderer availability as rules inputs.

## Evidence limits

This audit inspected local production source and the repository instructions/history/assets guide. It did not enumerate or reinterpret archive artwork, and no implementation, import, cleanup, or tests were run. Exact roster-to-art matching depends on the separate pack inventory and art-led 1:1 proposal reports. The SRD roster declares many properties and handlers, but its own provenance says `PARTIAL`; the complete current rules boundary for each entry should be checked against the source ledger before describing full stat-block parity.
