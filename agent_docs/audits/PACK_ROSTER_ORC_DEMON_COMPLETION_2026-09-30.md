## Shared native design calls

- **Ordinary wielded weapon:** use an actual existing item (`weapon.handaxe`, `weapon.battleaxe`, spear, sword, bow, shield, etc. as confirmed by a readable frame) and existing `Attack` resolution. Every row below chooses an actual item/action as a practical design proposal even where the art leaves subtype uncertain; that choice is never presented as an art fact. Don't install Dretch Bite/Claws as proxies for an obvious sword, axe, staff or bow.
- **Natural attack:** reserve `NaturalAttack` (native action template) for bodies visibly using claws, fangs, horns or limbs without held gear. Keep it independent of equipment slots. The current SRD Bite/Claw examples are reusable mechanics, but exact dice/type are not art facts.
- **Existing behaviors:** reusable existing actions include Goblin Nimble Escape, SRD Orc Aggressive, Multiattack, Pack Tactics, natural Bite and other SRD handlers documented in `dnd/monsters/SRD_TRAIT_IMPLEMENTATION_MAP.md`. An asset having multiple Attack clips does not grant Multiattack. Reuse only where chosen role and rules justify it.
- **Demon base:** Dretch is a partial, small CR foundation whose Bite and Claws are intrinsic weapon item grants (`weapon.creature.dretch_bite` and `weapon.creature.dretch_claws`), wired into configured Multiattack; it is not the generic `NaturalAttack` action and not a universal demon foundation. For a one-claw project demon, explicitly remove the intrinsic Bite/Claws and Multiattack grants, then use the existing NaturalAttack action template with authored claw facts, or deliberately retain the exact Dretch kit when it fits. Corrosive/Dread blood are real project mechanics from `dnd/monsters/demon_variants.py`, but art color/effects alone do not justify either.
- **Armed demon base:** use an existing humanoid martial composition (Guard/Bandit/Veteran or Orc where the role fits) as mechanical scaffolding and author a project fiend adaptation with real weapon possessions; keep its creature identity/traits separate from visual source identity. Fiend type, resistance, innate spellcasting, magical weapons and special effects require explicit content choices. Current art does not settle them.
- **Magic-like attack effects:** a rendered energy crescent or glowing hand supports “strikes with an authored visual effect” as a flavor description. It does not tell whether the cause is a magical weapon, spell, ranged ray, magical slashing damage, or detached effect. No spell/action follows automatically.
- **Elite:** rows proposing elite looks mean optional recolor/cosmetic presentation by default. If harder combat is wanted, author explicit HP/armor/stat changes or a fitting existing reusable ability. “Elite” in the vendor family name is not a rules feature.

### Implemented native mechanisms behind the proposals

The proposed ordinary items are real current definitions: dagger/handaxe/spear/battleaxe/longsword/mace/shortbow/shield are declared in `dnd/content/items/authored_item_definitions.py:279–305,354–395,464–475,752–755`; do not confuse an image overlay with inventory/equipment state. `action.monster.natural_attack` is registered in `dnd/content_system/action_definitions.py:196`, and `NaturalAttack` owns its damage/range values without representing carried gear in `dnd/monsters/traits.py:584–610`. The canonical Goblin Archer loadout is visibly shortbow plus scimitar and offhand dagger in `dnd/monsters/bestiary.py:311–414`, so the bow-only proposals explicitly remove the scimitar and offhand dagger grants. Goblin Caster and classless generic caster builders exist in `dnd/monsters/bestiary.py:420–590`; they register broader spell lists and the generic default loadout, so a restrained NPC proposal must curate those existing grants. The SRD Mage has Fire Bolt/Magic Missile among a much broader spell list and carries a dagger (`dnd/monsters/srd_roster.py:728–762`); this roster proposal deliberately uses the classless bespoke helpers for low-tier caster art instead. The Dretch partial block and Dretch intrinsic Bite/Claws/Multiattack declarations are in `dnd/monsters/srd_roster.py:1056–1074,1333–1337`. The proposed claw uses the separate `NaturalAttack` template, not those intrinsic weapon items. The full SRD Wolf block is the selected Animal 1/2 composition; the mounted wolf-like steed Bite remains a design suggestion, not asserted to be an implemented rider relation.

No firearm is evidenced in these 65 rows and none is proposed. The only firearm-like implementation found in the broader native audit is an environmental arcane machine-gun device; it is not a held weapon/loadout or NPC firearm combat system. Likewise the Orc/Goblin/Demon fixed art uses a fixed composite rig: riders cannot become independently targetable from pixels alone. Neither observation authorizes introducing a new global equipment, class, or mount framework.

This pass corrects the significant earlier overgeneralization that every Demon Spawn/Imp can inherit Dretch bite/claws. The armored, shielded, sword, spear, dagger, staff-like or bow-bearing figures need real possessions and ordinary weapon actions. Several glow/robe variants have caster-like gestures; the listed Fire Bolt/Magic Missile choices are explicit reusable design suggestions, never spells inferred from imagery.
