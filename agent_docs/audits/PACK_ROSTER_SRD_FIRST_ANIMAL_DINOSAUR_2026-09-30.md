# SRD-first art roster matching: animals, dinosaurs, and fixed humanoids (2026-09-30)

## Purpose and source hierarchy

This report replaces the earlier custom-first mechanics proposals for the 94-row scope only. The companion visual report remains authoritative for source archive paths, exact variant keys, observed equipment, chosen full 15-frame East attack strips, and palette/baked-armor facts. Its earlier NativeAttack choices are superseded visual-design suggestions, not evidence for the SRD-first mechanics here: [animal/dinosaur completion evidence](PACK_ROSTER_ANIMAL_DINOSAUR_COMPLETION_2026-09-30.md). This report adds a best-fit SRD creature/NPC chassis for each row, or states no fit where a forced match would mislead. SRD source identity and local implementation readiness are separate: every named source is eligible for selection even where repository status says `missing`. No complete new balanced block is authored here.

Source priority is the full official [SRD 5.1 PDF](https://media.wizards.com/2023/downloads/dnd/SRD_CC_v5.1.pdf), checked against all 317 creature entries and printed-page locators in `content_data/ledgers/srd_5_1_source_coverage.json` and the extracted 5.1 page corpus at `.runtime/pack-study-20260930/srd-first/SRD5.1-pages.json`. Where 5.1 has no honest candidate, this study considered the official [SRD 5.2](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.pdf) extracted pages `.runtime/pack-study-20260930/srd-first/SRD5.2-pages.json`, especially its additional beast/dinosaur entries (5.2 pp341–361). The dinosaur source is `/mnt/c/Users/tommaso/Documents/assets/smallscale/2D Dinosaurs Pack 1.rar`; the companion audit records the byte-identical-size Downloads copy and matching member listings without claiming hashes or byte equality. A 5.2 candidate is explicitly secondary and requires conversion to 5.1 action, save, condition, grapple/prone, spellcasting/slot, and timing semantics before use; it is not a drop-in block. Printed pages below mean page numbers printed on source, not PDF offsets.

Fit classes: **exact** = same named creature and close role; **cosmetic** = same SRD chassis with source color/sex/name variation; **loadout** = suitable statblock but source gear requires explicit replacement; **adapted** = meaningful base mechanics retained with named, bounded changes; **no_fit** = no SRD chassis is sufficiently truthful; keep custom passive-stat composition and art-led chosen NaturalAttack rather than force an unrelated block. Every role, gear/action selection, and preserved signature is a proposal grounded in source identity and companion visual evidence, not proof that the runtime already supports it. Every selected new or replacement weapon, armor, shield, and barding item receives the necessary explicit proficiency grant in the content proposal, including armor and shield proficiencies for the armored humanoids and dinosaur barding. This follows the SRD 5.1 monster-construction guidance (p.254), even where the selected NPC chassis does not print a player-style proficiency list. `NaturalAttack` is an existing content action family where mentioned; exact range/outcome/presentation readiness remains separate. Elite palettes alone do not grant mechanics.

The source pages establish the summarized signatures below. The chosen strip NaturalAttack in the companion report is visual/design evidence only; it cannot delete any selected SRD attack, trait, rider, or movement rule. If a canonical source action has no matching clip, record a presentation gap and retain the SRD action. Exact animation inspection is documented once in the companion report: 55 animal attack strips, 21 dinosaur attack strips, and all fixed-humanoid visible-gear/action clips were visually reviewed. Rows below point back to that variant-specific evidence rather than duplicating contact-sheet descriptions. For damage/contact uncertainty, the companion report distinguishes observed motion from chosen gameplay attack.

Use only the actually visible carried equipment, with conservative half plate as an armor-subtype design choice only where protective armor is visibly present but cannot be identified. The source weapon/armor list in SRD 5.1 is considered independently of local catalog availability.
