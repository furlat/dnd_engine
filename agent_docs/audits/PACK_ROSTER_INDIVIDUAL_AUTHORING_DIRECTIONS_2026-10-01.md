# Individual fixed-character authoring directions

Date:1 October2026. Scope:150 retained non-animal variants. No engine/art import changes. User direction: existing modular sprites cover ordinary humanoids and skeletons; their fixed-art counterparts are special characters. Goblins, Orcs, Devils and Zombies must cover the core SRD faction creatures as well as special variants.

SRD5.1 foundations remain the starting point. Each card states actual-gear treatment, power benchmark, a concrete signature proposal, team role and counterplay. Signatures are design additions, not visual facts or implemented capabilities. Shared source animations honestly share maneuver families; a unique name alone does not imply unique magic. Final CR/XP requires recalculation after gear, HP, action or damage changes. The15 core faction rows keep ordinary source abilities and do not acquire invented signatures.

Plan/self-reviews: review original art, separate evidence from item choices, author per-character direction, preserve core faction coverage, integrate gallery, check source/profile consistency. Anti-slop self-review checks physical hand use/action costs/targeting and distinguishes source rules from additions. Anti-OOP/ECS self-review confirms passive records and standalone HTML only; no engine framework or implementation. Neither is independent approval.
