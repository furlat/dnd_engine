# Authored Devils: 34 artwork variants and level 1–12 teams

Date: 1 October 2026. Status: proposed creature compositions, ready for gallery
integration and human review. Numerical values and CR targets are provisional;
no encounter or creature balance has been playtested. This document changes
design only. It does not install art or implement creatures.

## Human direction and bounded plan

The human chose **Devils** for the whole vendor Demon pack, prohibited borrowing
Humanoid NPC stat blocks for it, required visible equipment to control loadouts,
and asked for enemies across **player levels 1 through 12**, including substantial
opponents and teams whose abilities work together. These are player levels,
not twelve invented monster levels or automatic scaling of a single grunt.

The bounded plan is to inspect bodies and carried gear, propose genuine SRD
Devils where they fit, author the remaining creatures, crosswalk all 34 literal
source keys, and design one team for each player level. Anti-slop reviewer and
anti-OOP/ECS reviewer passes are included below as **author self-reviews**;
they are not independent approvals. The gallery chat owns integration into the
existing roster tables, coverage manifest and HTML.

The public-facing faction is **Devils**. The archive name, `demons` inventory
key, filenames and literal variant keys remain provenance. In particular,
`Demon spawn 1` keeps its lowercase `spawn`. Renaming conceptual content does
not authorize renaming source artwork or existing rig identifiers.

## Fresh inspection and SRD selection

Evidence comes from the preserved `2D Demons - TopDown assetpack v1.1.zip`, the
gallery's verified South-facing Idle thumbnails, and fresh East-facing source
Attack probes. All 34 bodies were inspected beside multiple Attack frames;
full Attack 1–4 strips were also inspected for the disputed archers, casters,
winged bodies and `Demon Spawn 13`. Private contact sheets and strips are linked
[here](../../.runtime/pack-study-20260930/srd-first/illustration-comparison/root-devil-authorship-evidence/body-and-attack-0.png),
[here](../../.runtime/pack-study-20260930/srd-first/illustration-comparison/root-devil-authorship-evidence/body-and-attack-1.png),
and [here](../../.runtime/pack-study-20260930/srd-first/illustration-comparison/root-devil-authorship-evidence/body-and-attack-2.png).

The review found four clear bow users (`Demon Spawn 6`, `7`, `10`, `Imp 7`),
four staff users (`Demon spawn 1`, `Demon Spawn 8`, `11`, `Imp 9`), paired melee
weapons on `Demon Spawn 13`, paired curved blades on `Demon Elite 2`, and bat
wings on `Demon Beast 5`. These observations supersede contrary earlier kit
claims. `Imp 3` has a carried/back-mounted standard, not a huge biological crest.
`Imp 6` uses a spear. Broad item class is usually clearer than exact subtype;
the crosswalk distinguishes inspection from a chosen SRD item representation.

Only **two** proposed rows retain a named SRD Devil foundation: winged,
horned Imp candidates with added equipment. Their tails/Sting were not resolved
in the inspected poses and remain a specific visual-fit question, alongside
alternate-form rig support. Both are **loadout adaptations**,
not unchanged or exact matches. The other **32** rows receive independently
authored Fiend (Devil) profiles. None uses a Guard, Thug, Spy, Berserker, Knight,
Veteran, Bandit Captain or Mage stat block. Ordinary SRD weapons, spells and
combat actions remain usable rules components; they are not NPC chassis.

Rejected comparisons are explicit:

- Dretch, Quasit, Hezrou and Vrock are Demon profiles. Changing their subtype
  would not make these new Devil identities genuine matches. Vrock also has a
  bird/beak/talon body that these bat-winged bipeds lack.
- Barbed Devil is not selected solely because a body has a few spikes; Bearded
  Devil is not selected solely because a soldier carries a polearm. Their
  signature anatomy/actions were not established.
- Bone Devil's skeletal/stinger body, Chain Devil's chains and Ice Devil's
  insect-like anatomy are absent. Marilith's six arms and serpentine lower body
  are absent. These names do not describe generic armed bipeds.
- Horned Devil is a comparison for the large flyers, not their chosen block;
  its complete weapon/body/action combination was not established.
- Erinyes is not selected for the grounded armored soldiers. The actual 5.1
  block **does contain Parry** and does not contain Devil's Sight; the earlier
  review's claim that it lacks Parry was incorrect. Rejecting this match does
  not justify misreporting its source rules.
- `Imp 7` is a grounded archer, not a flying Imp. A vendor family name alone
  never grants Imp shapechanging, poison sting or invisibility.

Primary creature reference: [official SRD 5.1](https://media.wizards.com/2023/downloads/dnd/SRD_CC_v5.1.pdf),
Devils pp.274–279, Imp pp.277–278. No 5.2 creature profile is selected for this
Devil draft. This preserves the human's 5.1 priority and conversion boundary.

## Shared authored choices and rules boundaries

All C profiles below are authored Fiends (Devils), lawful evil, speaking
Infernal and Common. Deliberate faction choices give them fire and poison
immunity, immunity to being poisoned, and darkvision 60 ft. These are chosen
lore/rules features, **not deductions from red pixels**. They do not receive
automatic physical resistance, telepathy, shapechanging, invisibility, Devil's
Sight or Magic Resistance. Only explicitly listed profiles receive extra traits.

Abilities are listed in STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA order. Hit-point averages are
rounded down. AC uses one stated formula and any real shield; armor is not
stacked with natural armor. All authored creatures are proficient with their
listed worn armor, shields and weapons. Listed attack bonuses include their
stated PB. Size is a deliberate encounter/scene choice, not feet measured from
sprite pixels. Small equipment users avoid Heavy weapons. Large body attacks
use deliberately authored dice; carried items never gain extra dice just from
an imposing pose.

Melee attacks have reach 5 ft unless stated otherwise. Each attack targets one
creature. Explicit Multiattack entries are one action; paired weapons do not
add a free third attack. Listed spells retain 5.1 casting times, targeting,
durations, saving throws and concentration. Authored innate spellcasting uses
Charisma and waives material components only; verbal/somatic requirements
remain. A sword-and-shield caster must free its sword hand before a somatic
cast. Two-handed staffs/bows can be held in one hand while casting, but cannot
be used for a two-handed attack with the other hand occupied.

**Bolt** is an authored ranged spell attack, range 60 ft, dealing the explicitly
listed force dice. It is neither a carried weapon nor an inferred Fire Bolt.
**Infernal Weapons**, where listed, makes those weapon attacks magical and adds
the stated necrotic dice on a hit; it does not turn the items into magical loot.
Claws/gore are intrinsic body attacks. Other natural attacks become magical
only if explicitly listed. Colored cast/slash FX proves neither damage type nor
a particular spell. All attack-marker, flight, component, spell and alternate-
form rig bindings remain presentation work; no clip supplies a contact time
merely by being named Attack.

**Blade Parry** is an authored reaction: against one melee attack from a visible
attacker, while holding a melee weapon, increase AC by the stated amount for
that attack. **Shield Cover** is an authored reaction: before the attack roll,
when a visible attacker within 5 ft makes a melee attack against another ally
within 5 ft, impose disadvantage on that one roll while the devil holds its
shield. It grants no taunt, interception teleport or protection from area/ranged
effects. These definitions are separate composition proposals, not a claim of
existing native support.

### Two genuine Imp foundations with equipment changes

**I01 Shieldwing Imp** (`Imp 1`) and **I02 Reaping Imp** (`Demon Spawn 3`) use
the complete 5.1 Imp profile from pp.277–278. Keep its Tiny Devil/shapechanger
identity, source abilities/HP, flight, defenses, senses, social skills, traits,
Sting including its poison save, Invisibility and three alternate forms. Their
alternate forms and Sting-specific presentation are unresolved rig bindings;
missing artwork does not silently delete source rules.

I01 adds an explicitly proficient Mace and shield, representing the short
hafted weapon and shield. Its normal-form AC becomes 15; Mace attack is +0,
1d6−2 bludgeoning, minimum 0. I02 adds an explicitly proficient Sickle for the
solid curved blade on a haft, not a speculative flexible whip. AC stays 13;
Sickle attack is +0, 1d4−2 slashing, minimum 0. Neither weapon is Finesse, so
the source Imp's Dexterity does not replace Strength for these attacks. These
weak carried attacks do not replace its intrinsic Sting. Normal-form gear
must be retained separately across shapechanging and counted for AC only when
the active form can actually use it. Tiny scene scale is a chosen binding.

Source CR1 is a benchmark for both; added gear makes their final CR provisional.
They are **loadout** matches and never counted as exact/cosmetic source matches.

## A team for every player level 1–12

The planning assumption is **four adventurers of the listed level**. These are
encounter candidates using the provisional CR targets above. Use the 2014/5.1
encounter procedure, not 5.2's replacement budgets: sum XP and apply the
multiple-creature adjustment. Difficulty below is conditional on the targets
being validated; terrain, resources and coordination can change it. The
adjusted XP is a difficulty estimate, not awarded XP. Supporting units listed
here materially contribute attacks/control/protection and count in that estimate.

There are six distinct substantial authored officer/boss designs at target
CR5–12, several CR3–6 beasts/specialists, and a deliberately small CR1/8–1
entry group. This is not a faction made only of weaklings. The two source Imp
loadouts are optional infiltrators, not the default level-1 encounter: their
retained source sting has substantial poison damage.

Additional squads reuse the same compositions instead of inventing bonuses:

- **Darkness hunting party:** C18 Night Notary + C17 Night Archer + C05 Huntsman
  Wing all explicitly have Devil's Sight. Notary chooses Darkness and therefore
  cannot also concentrate on Web/Slow. The other Devils do not automatically
  see in magical darkness. Dispel/light interactions and caster pressure can
  break the formation; casts requiring sight still need real visibility.
- **Captain's line:** C10 Oath Captain + C09 Glaive Warden + C20 Ivory Marksman.
  Bless is the captain's deliberate support action; it helps attack/save rolls
  and requires concentration. Only the actual shield carrier uses Shield Cover.
- **Assault breach:** C11 Gate Tyrant + C06 Oathblade. Tyrant may trade one axe
  attack for an ordinary Shove to set up close melee advantage against prone.
  This is a strong squad to budget separately, not a free add-on to a lower-level
  fight. Thunderwave also affects eligible allies in its area.

Across the wider roster, keep source-matched animals/goblins/undead/humanoids as
alternative factions at their own suitable encounter tiers. These Devil teams
already provide an explicit 1–12 coverage floor. A Lich keeps its genuine high
source power and belongs beyond this default progression; do not weaken it to
fill a missing mid-tier slot. Modern zombie exclusions supply no enemy count
or encounter budget. Optional zombie backstories supply no synergies or powers.

## Anti-slop reviewer — author self-review

- All 34 source keys are retained once in the crosswalk. There are 32 authored
  profiles and two separately identified SRD Imp loadouts, with zero exact
  creature-match claims and no Humanoid NPC source blocks.
- Bow/staff/paired-weapon/wing corrections come from fresh frame inspection.
  Unresolved item heads and chosen armor subtypes stay explicit; source labels,
  colors and FX grant no unlisted mechanics.
- The former Demon substitutions, false crest/empty-hand claims, wrong Elite 2
  shield and incorrect Erinyes-Parry review conclusion are not carried forward.
- Player levels 1–12 each have an explicit team and conditional XP arithmetic;
  higher ranks have stronger owned numeric/action profiles, not cosmetic elite
  names. CR targets and encounter bands remain unvalidated design values.
- Team rules account for action/reaction cost, one concentration effect per
  caster, Help scope, actual reach, prone versus ranged attacks, friendly area
  effects and the absence of automatic Devil's Sight on every profile.
- These are self-checks of the proposal. The older independent approvals do not
  validate these new creatures, their balance or their visual interpretations.

## Anti-OOP/ECS reviewer — author self-review

Each profile describes composed ability/health/defense/sense/equipment/action
data and shared rule capabilities. Source variant, display name, faction and
rig binding are presentation/provenance fields, not one entity subclass per
sprite. Items are actual possessed/equipped data; baked pixels suppress only
duplicate rendering. Innate claws are body actions, not inventory weapons.

No generic creature factory, registry, dynamic attribute access, late imports,
type-check dispatch or new inheritance hierarchy is proposed. Native actions,
spell applications, turn/reaction economy and condition ownership must remain
with their existing systems. Team behavior chooses legal actions and positions;
it is not a second combat executor or a passive faction buff engine. Legendary
economy and the two authored reactions are finite rules requirements that need
native ownership/support verified before implementation. They are not implemented
by this document or hidden in renderer callbacks.

Private image probes are evidence only. No originals were renamed, moved or
overwritten, no production rig was imported, and no gameplay test was needed
for this documentation pass. Engine implementation and balance/playback testing
are a subsequent authorized unit.
