# Independent anti-OOP/ECS review: art-led NPC roster

**Historical review snapshot:** this approval applies to the earlier art/composition
study. It does not approve the subsequent SRD-first rematch. The
[reconciled synthesis](../ART_LED_NPC_ROSTER_DESIGN_2026-09-30.md) identifies the
current tables and fresh review records.

Date: 30 September 2026  
Scope: independent architecture and ownership review of the 259-variant art-led roster design and its completion evidence. Read-only review; no production code, data, imports, tests, or source reports changed.

## Result

**Final review: approved as a content study and design boundary, with the limitations below explicit.** The proposal treats each art variant as a traceable roster row rather than an engine type. It composes passive creature facts, actual possessions, existing actions/conditions/handlers, and authored appearance/presentation data. Distinct mechanics are proposed only where the current engine lacks a fitting owner, and those remain design proposals. The reports do not authorize implementation or claim production support.

The completion records collectively cover 259 fixed variant identities: 65 Orc/Goblin/Demon, 100 Undead/Enemy/Zombie, 73 Animals/Barbarian/Character, and 21 Dinosaurs. This count belongs in study documentation and source evidence. It does not call for runtime archive scans, audit systems, or one engine type per art entry.

## ECS and ownership findings

- **Creature composition stays in native content.** The native audit points to existing bestiary/SRD factories, passive blocks, possession grants, item definitions, and action/condition owners. The roster selects or adapts those compositions. It does not introduce NPC subclasses, a generic NPC framework, per-sprite executors, a second content registry, or application-level encounter branches. Modular humanoid builds and modular `NakedBody2` skeletons remain their existing richer path; fixed Undead proposals stay separate.
- **Equipment and intrinsic attacks retain different meanings.** Clear carried weapons are proposed as actual items and ordinary attacks. Animal anatomy and selected demon/monster claws, fangs, horns, and similar attacks use the existing `NaturalAttack` action configuration without inventing lootable gear. Empty-handed humanoid proposals say ordinary unarmed strike where that is intended. Dretch's Bite/Claws are intrinsic item grants with separate Multiattack wiring; the completion report explicitly removes those grants and Multiattack when a single custom Claw action is selected. A future implementation must compose or replace inherited grants deliberately and select Dretch resistances/traits individually; using Dretch as a scaffold does not imply every Dretch block belongs in the result.
- **NaturalAttack has bounded demonstrated semantics.** The existing action owns natural dice/type/range and range/line-of-sight checks, and routes its result through attack resolution. `natural_range` can express `RangeType.RANGE`, but the default `weapon_slot` is `MELEE_MAIN`; that proxy is passed into outcome-profile construction and the `AttackEvent`. Equipment attack/damage baselines and some handlers branch on the slot. Therefore ranged metadata alone does not prove ranged attack bonuses, discovery, delivery, or slot-sensitive behavior are correct. The reports now leave Toxic Spit as a pending candidate until those owners and presentation are checked. This is an evidence limit, not a request to add a parallel attack executor.
- **Canonical loadouts are protected.** The roster preserves the canonical Goblin, Goblin Archer, Skeleton Warrior, and SRD entries as existing content. Incompatible grants are stripped or replaced in the proposed derived composition: for example the bow-only Goblin drops the canonical Archer's `weapon.scimitar` and off-hand `weapon.dagger`; the fixed DeathLord proposal does not inherit the Skeleton Warrior's unpictured flask; and fixed Skeleton Archer proposals omit its twin daggers. This is authored variant composition, not mutation of canonical SRD data. The reports accurately call foundations partial where their own provenance says so.
- **Stable identity and presentation remain separate.** Native rules do not depend on source folder names, pixels, media paths, frame names, installed-art readiness, or renderer execution. Stable creature identity, permitted appearance facts, and admitted possession/action facts can select presentation through the existing `AnimationData.creature_rigs` and `actor_contact` direction. Existing legacy presentation metadata is acknowledged without turning this study into a global migration.
- **Retained history remains the playback authority.** The design binds from admitted retained facts and preceding actor state, keeps complete causal lineages and independent latest/history behavior, and rejects querying live `Entity`/`GridMap` state to redraw history. It does not flatten reactions or add a parallel render-event taxonomy. Unsupported disarm, cast, or alternate-loadout visuals are stated as presentation gaps rather than used to invalidate or rewrite native rules.
- **Rig adaptation is a suitable small data boundary.** The proposal resolves the existing semantic recipe/profile first, then allows passive presentation data to associate the selected rig and action identity with a compatible clip and action-specific anchor overrides. Attacks retain recipe plus `AttackVariant.id`; casts and generic actions retain their own recipe identity. One shared resolver must serve all these consumers while each existing recipe remains the source of its anchor vocabulary. A `BodyClip`-only anchor map would be insufficient where actions share a clip or need different markers. The current typed `BodyRig`, `BodyClip`, `AttackVariant`, cast draft, and body-action recipe are appropriate building blocks; no generic animation graph or action executor is justified.
- **Timing respects current semantics.** The design preserves body duration `(frames - 1) * 1000 / (fps * speed)` and marker time `frame * 1000 / (fps * speed)`, and treats the final sampled index as the end. Variable six-, seven-, fifteen-, and sixteen-frame clips are supported as authored clip facts; any later marker type extension should validate against the selected clip rather than impose a fifteen-frame convention. Vendor clip names do not define native action count, contact, or release timing.
- **Layer ownership is clear.** Separate body, ground-shadow, and effect tracks are preferred where source evidence supports them; paired combined sheets are not drawn with a second shadow. The shadow audit demonstrates exact recomposition only for the named GreyWolf and SkeletonArcher05 clip pairs. It makes no global extraction claim and does not introduce runtime shadow inference.

## Named limitations, not architecture blockers

- Carried firearms have no established native item/reload/ammunition/action composition. Shooter, Sniper, and five Zombie Soldier proposals retain firearm identity and explicitly identify shared firearm content as required before faithful implementation. This is an honest future content gap; the environmental arcane machine gun is not repurposed as carried gear and crossbows are not substituted.
- The seven firearm rows now have a concrete shared carried-longgun design proposal (carbine, precision rifle, or service rifle role), with authored projectile attacks and no crossbow/device substitution. The item/action owner and execution compatibility are still unimplemented and require a later content capability check. Thus the roster has selected a matching concept without claiming the seven rows are currently playable.
- The five ELITE Armored dinosaur proposals use the existing `armor.half_plate` wearable as a conservative large-creature barding analogue. The worn possession supplies gameplay armor facts; the fixed sprite already contains the armor pixels, so presentation must suppress only a duplicate overlay. Existing wearable definitions, possession builders, and AC composition are fitting content owners. Medium-body-armor semantics, size/wearability, Dexterity cap, and effective AC remain unverified authoring decisions; the report does not claim final balance or compatibility.
- The targeted armor reconciliation now selects actual existing `armor.plate` for Goblin 15, Orc 12, Demon Elite 2/5/6, and Demon Spawn 7, replacing any inherited/scaffold armor grants. Demon Beast 3 explicitly has no worn armor; its proposed intrinsic defense belongs in passive AC facts and strips Dretch armor. Undead DarkKnight selects existing `armor.half_plate` and replaces inherited scraps; DeathLord explicitly keeps its existing armor scraps. The fixed Character Knight, Paladin, and DeathKnight each select existing `armor.half_plate` as a conservative worn-item choice. These are appropriate current owners: `WearableDefinition`/item builders and possession grants for gear, and existing `EquipmentConfig`/armor-class composition for passive intrinsic AC. Item subtype and AC are authored proposals, not pixel evidence; baked pixels stay visible while duplicate item overlays are suppressed. Body-size/wearability and final AC/equipment compatibility still need later content validation. These refinements improve kit completeness and do not create a new ECS boundary or require an armor framework.
- Mounted Goblin art remains a baked rider-and-steed composite. The study keeps rider and steed concepts distinct and reports that no native riding composer or independent composite playback was found. It does not fake a native relationship in the renderer. This is an unresolved capability for those variants only.
- Reused creature foundations are partial scaffolds, not assertions of full SRD parity. Spell lists, elite tuning, flight, damage types, special effects, and optional new riders remain explicit authored choices. Vendor action names and visual effects do not silently grant those mechanics.
- The proposed per-rig action binding is a design direction, not a settled schema. Key placement and representation should be decided only in a later, specifically authorized implementation plan. Source FPS, pivots, contact/release frames, and action compatibility require evidence-led authoring; the reports do not claim visual completeness.

## Review boundary

I reread the stable parent design and five focused completion/audit reports, the anti-slop review, repository instructions/history, and the cited production owners. For the ranged-natural-attack limitation I inspected `NaturalAttack` in `dnd/monsters/traits.py`, its root-owned action declaration, and attack baseline/range handling in `dnd/actions.py` and `dnd/blocks/equipment.py`. For worn and intrinsic armor I checked `WearableDefinition`, item builders/possession composition, `EquipmentConfig`, and armor-class ownership. Findings support the proposed study boundary and current ownership direction. This approval does not approve exact balance, pixel identification of selected armor subtypes, firearm execution, large-creature barding compatibility/effective AC, production bindings, renderer behavior, a future schema, or native implementation. No additional gate is required to finish this study beyond the separate anti-slop disposition and final status reconciliation.
