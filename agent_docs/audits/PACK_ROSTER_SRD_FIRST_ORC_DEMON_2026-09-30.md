# SRD-first Orc, Goblin, and Demon roster rematch

Date: 2026-09-30. Design study only; production content and the earlier art/native studies are unchanged. This companion records proposed mechanical profile selection. `PACK_ROSTER_ORC_DEMON_COMPLETION_2026-09-30.md` remains archive provenance only; its earlier Demon body and gear claims are superseded for the Devil pack by `PACK_ROSTER_AUTHORED_DEVILS_DRAFT_2026-10-01.md` and the Devil rows below. Source-family strings below are exact join keys, not mechanical content IDs.

## Method and interpretation

The primary comparison is the complete SRD 5.1 creature profile, not the subset already implemented locally. A selected profile keeps its printed creature type, chassis, signature traits/actions, senses, movement, resistances, spellcasting, and action count unless a row explicitly identifies a gear-driven change. `fit` means: **exact** = no meaningful mismatch; **cosmetic** = profile retained with appearance-only difference; **loadout** = selected profile is retained but visible gear requires explicit equipment/action edits; **adapted** = a named source profile is consciously used for this art/role despite consequential body, type, or equipment mismatch; **authored** = a new non-SRD creature profile is proposed with no SRD creature chassis; **no_fit** = no SRD source is a defensible base and no authored replacement is included in that row. None of these labels claims balanced encounter CR after edits.

Art facts are drawn from the direct archive inspection documented in the companion art completion report, including Combined body Attack 1 strips and mounted/animal action sheets. For row-by-row visual provenance, the exact source-family key in this table joins to the identically named row of `PACK_ROSTER_ORC_DEMON_COMPLETION_2026-09-30.md`, which records ZIP member path, source facing/row and selected frames; this SRD rematch does not claim a second archive enumeration. Uncertain subtype reads are marked as a design choice. The selected SRD identity is not inferred from the art; it is the proposed full rules source. No new signature spell, resistance, flight, multiattack or condition is inferred from a glow, palette, motion or vendor family label. A proposed equipment addition is real SRD gear, and its changed AC/attack is stated. Where gear is new or replaced, the loadout explicitly grants weapon, armor and shield proficiency needed to use it, consistent with SRD 5.1 monster-stat guidance (p.254); this grant is a content choice, not a pixel inference. **Worn armor possessions are an explicit design decision separate from baked pixels**; where no armor is shown the row removes armor and recalculates AC. Shields add +2. Weapon math uses the selected source ability modifier/proficiency and ordinary SRD weapon properties. Changed weapon/armor makes source CR a benchmark only; encounter tuning remains open. All selected plate-wearing profiles have STR 15 or higher, so their proposed plate kits do not incur the SRD heavy-armor low-Strength speed penalty.

Primary sources: [SRD 5.1 PDF](https://media.wizards.com/2023/downloads/dnd/SRD_CC_v5.1.pdf), released under CC-BY-4.0, and [SRD 5.2 PDF](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.pdf) (5.2.0). The primary roster uses SRD 5.1. The sole 5.2 comparison is Goblin Minion p.287 as a secondary visual/loadout analogue for the two-dagger Goblin; no 5.2 type, statistics, traits or action format are carried across. No 5.2 creature block is selected over an available 5.1 match.

## Reusable exact-profile selection vs local implementation

The non-Devil rows retain the creature profiles named in their entries. The Devil rows below replace all previous fiend mappings: 32 authored Fiends and two SRD Imp loadout candidates. The earlier candidate list and local-availability notes are historical context only; the standalone Devil draft gives the active profile details, rejected comparisons and implementation boundaries. No Humanoid NPC chassis is selected for this pack.

Local availability is not parity for the retained non-Devil SRD rows. Check each complete selected trait/stat block against the engine source ledger before implementation. None of the authored Devil profiles is installed, and the two Imp candidates still require full source-profile and alternate-form binding work. The Devil draft is design-only and does not certify support or balance.

Caster proposals use the full 5.1 Mage spell list, slots, traits and action set, not the prior two-spell shortlist or a PC class build. Goblin/Orc ancestry is an explicit composition choice; do not stack all racial stat blocks onto Mage. Mage remains Humanoid unless content authors deliberately choose another type. `dnd/monsters/bestiary.py`'s classless `create_caster`/`create_goblin_caster` scaffolds are not SRD Mage equivalents and have their own configurable curated spell/item choices; `dnd/content/characters/builds.py` owns actual player class builds.

Goblin 11/12 are **mounted-rider** rows. The art shows rider and quadruped persisting through idle, attack, run and death strips; design is separate Goblin rider + Worg steed. SRD 5.1 Mounted Combat p.99 allows a controlled mount only Dash, Disengage or Dodge, while an independent mount retains its actions. The proposed Worg Bite therefore depends on the selected independent/controlled mount relationship. Local native code search found no riding/mount/dismount owner. The fixed composite artwork also cannot alone provide independent targeting or split death/turn ownership.

## Shared reusable mechanics and explicit gaps

The minimal shared mechanics needed by these proposals are already ordinary native content: real equipment possessions and ordinary weapon attack resolution; shield/armor AC composition; existing Goblin Nimble Escape and Orc Aggressive; Bugbear Brute/Surprise Attack; Wolf/Worg Bite with its printed prone rider; Mage spellcasting data/actions for the non-Devil rows; authored Devil profile content as specified in the linked Devil draft; and already-authored source-profile trait handlers where present. These do not call for a roster framework. Exact missing full fiend profiles/features are content gaps, not evidence to approximate authored Devils with another creature chassis.

Natural attacks are intrinsic selected-profile actions and do not occupy inventory or hand slots. Thus an Imp with a visible dagger still retains Sting, and an armed creature can still retain its natural action when its selected full SRD profile has one; Dretch claws are never used as a proxy for a visible sword/axe/bow. No fixed-roster row shows a firearm; none is proposed. The unrelated `environment.arcane_machine_gun` is an environmental spell-device, not carried firearm equipment or NPC firearm rules.

A new mount/rider relationship is the clearest possible shared mechanic candidate, needed only for the two mounted Goblins: controlled-versus-independent mount action policy, shared movement/space, mount/dismount, rider/steed targeting and death ownership. The repository study found no existing native riding owner, so this is an unresolved future capability question, not an implementation recommendation in this report. The fixed art can continue as a composite visual only if content/runtime semantics explicitly decide how the two rules entities are represented.

No other new mechanic is required to explain the observed gear. Elite variants are not automatically a harder rule tier: retain the selected base profile’s full signature features and tune HP/AC/encounter role deliberately after the visible gear changes; do not add a one-off “elite” power from a filename. Caster effects remain spell choices of the selected full Mage profile, not image-derived spell identifications.

## Uncertainties and source checks

- Pixel evidence establishes held-object broad class more often than precise subtype. The table selects ordinary legal items conservatively and labels that selection separately from the observation.
- Full profile gear changes can affect CR, attack bonus, damage, AC or type. The row states material changes but does not recalculate encounter CR, HP tuning or all secondary derived values; these are later content balancing decisions.
- The local ledger is an implementation-status inventory, not a rules-parity test. Native source declarations often say partial. Do not treat “playable” as complete SRD fidelity. Relevant owners: SRD creature facts/gear/trait wiring in `dnd/monsters/srd_roster.py:1077–1105,1200–1395`; Goblin/Nimble Escape in `dnd/monsters/bestiary.py:35–110`; actual Goblin Archer grants at `bestiary.py:311–414` (shortbow, scimitar, offhand dagger); NaturalAttack in `dnd/monsters/traits.py:584–700`; Brute/Surprise Attack at `traits.py:841–860`; Pack Tactics at `traits.py:281+`. These line anchors identify code owners, not an independent parity certification.
- Mounted rules are verified at SRD 5.1 printed p.99 in the prepared page text.
