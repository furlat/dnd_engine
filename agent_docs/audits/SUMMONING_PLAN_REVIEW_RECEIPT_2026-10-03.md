## Alternate-rig design reviewed

Current BodyRig/AnimationData registry remains the single rig authority. The
bounded extension has two distinct existing-owner responsibilities:

- Rig-scoped AttackProfileMatch rows select the exact intrinsic item/recorded
  source kind and existing actor/anchor/effect data. Ordinary intrinsic Weapon
  attacks keep their equipped discriminator; NATURAL access is a different
  mechanical property. No new attack runner or event reclassification.
- One passive per-rig body-context table covers the non-attack body assumptions
  that global human clips cannot supply. A pure binding helper reads retained
  facts and feeds the existing movement/condition/body-action/equipment owners.

ECS review R8 found that movement mode alone could not distinguish ordinary
walking, direct-arc jumps and connector traversal. The corrected selector uses
actual recorded mode/trajectory/connector identity, or an exact action/condition
reference, then at most one role-default and a verified compatible shared default.
No overlapping wildcard rules or row-order selection. Body markers, looping,
reversal, held poses and frame keys are explicit validated data.

The same resolved primary/recovery data is retained for timing and sampling;
Shove and forced movement are named actual consumers. A legitimate disabled
gesture preserves effect/child joins, while required attack/travel/pose coverage
cannot be hidden. Existing modular and four fixed-rig paths have regression
coverage. No actor/species branches, parallel registry or native lookups during
replay are introduced. All three reviewers approve these final refinements.
