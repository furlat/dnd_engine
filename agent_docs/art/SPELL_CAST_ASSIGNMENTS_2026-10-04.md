## Shared authoring and verification

Existing StudioCast/StudioActorLayer/PaletteTreatment records are selected by a generic offline command. It writes existing recipe fields; no runtime assignment registry. Source-hand media and projectile source sockets follow the selected pose. All unrelated Godot delivery/outcome data remains owned by its existing recipe. Original sheets are admitted through the verified asset installer.

Power budgets: quiet cantrips, focused levels1–2, empowered levels3–5, major levels6–9. Delivery clarity can reduce accent density; level does not mandate rings or skulls. Major Godot effects carry most of the spell progression.
