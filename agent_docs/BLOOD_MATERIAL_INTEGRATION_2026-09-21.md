# Blood-only damage material integration

The integration consumes `blood-material-lab/BLOOD-ONLY-HANDOFF.md` and its
`material.js` in the environment art worktree. The lab's spell programs are
excluded. Production spell trajectories, contacts, anchors and palettes remain
their existing authored programs.

Actual positive resolved damage selects the causal type. BodyReleaseResult now
retains optional primary and secondary damage types. A positive physical
component keeps the existing wound pattern; a nonphysical rider supplies only a
small edge accent. If physical damage was immune, the positive nonphysical
component becomes primary. These are factual damage values in the recorded
release, not backend animation instructions. Old recordings omit them and retain
the original presentation. Fresh captures are required to review the addition.

`game/data/blood-responses.json` maps all thirteen types. Bludgeoning, piercing
and slashing retain the existing approved presentation. The ten other entries
select shared droplet shapes, timing, palettes and finite detail. Only normal
blood consumes this table: skeleton fragments and demonic/corrosive material
retain their existing identity and behavior.

The High56 particles, stable identities, receiving ellipses and landing kernels
are reused. Motion modifiers recompute a trajectory to the same native goal;
they cannot create deposits on other tiles or beyond blockers. Force/thunder
footprint expansion from the study remains a proposal, not an implemented rule.
Frozen red pieces keep their identities after landing and melt into those same
kernels. Older contributions remain present during each new hit's flight/melt.

Vapor samples a particular droplet's past emission position and then rises and
fades independently. It is not a body burst or a follower attached to the target.
Poison has cohesive crimson beads and olive detail without vapor; necrotic has
dark ragged clots and muted vapor. Other shared detail follows the handoff.

Fresh floor color is finite and confined to the new contribution's share of the
actual merged field. Alpha and older-only pixels remain unchanged. It settles
to the ordinary retained red field, so later observers reconstruct the same
persistent floor from tile state alone. This does not add elemental tile
conditions or elemental wall coatings. The same landing schedule drives flight,
floor growth, cold melt and finite completion.

Anti-slop review: graphics_antislop GO. The bounded detail vocabulary follows
the handoff rather than spell-specific execution paths. Native metadata is
causal, region geometry is unchanged, and spell/source contacts are untouched.
ECS review: the earlier reviewed material plan and delivered handoff study
approve the passive response table, native factual metadata and existing
body-response owner; no particle entities or second simulation are introduced.

The initial validation passed 141 existing native/material/replay tests and ten
new actual-spell/particle tests. A direct pre/post comparison of 120 sampled
physical particle frames and 96 dagger, sword and mace ground rasters is
identical. Final integration and fresh gameplay evidence follow.


Final material review: [18 saved-event clips](http://127.0.0.1:8767/runs/20260921T001500Z-8cd69a/index.html)
all pass with no presentation gaps (2,176 frames, each containing all four cameras).
They cover Ray of Frost, Chill Touch, repeated Eldritch Blast, Acid Splash,
repeated Fireball, Sacred Flame, Shocking Grasp, Poison Spray and Thunderwave,
from both recorded observers. No spell events are generated by the renderer.
Cold flight/melt and poison flight/deposition were also inspected in extracted
frame sequences. Psychic is covered by the response/data tests; no new psychic
spell or projectile has been authored for this blood-only integration.

The combined integration run passed 426 tests. Its six existing expected
failures concern raised-floor/cliff and stair-displacement occlusion, outside
the fixed blade/crusher sprite-overlap issue. The selected typechecks are clean.
