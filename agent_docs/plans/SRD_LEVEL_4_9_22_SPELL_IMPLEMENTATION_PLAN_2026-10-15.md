## Scope and source policy

- Exactly **22 new spell roots**: one rank 1 and 21 rank 2 from the audit. By level: **5 / 4 / 5 / 3 / 2 / 3**. Creature recipes, granted actions, portable delivery modes and condition identities do not count as additional spells.
- Eligible spell identities and spell dependencies must be in **SRD 5.1 or SRD 5.2**. Preference for their implementation: **documented shipped BG 3 adaptation > 5.1 > 5.2**. BG 3 is not another eligible spell list. No mods, cut spells or renamed non-SRD spells.
- Feeblemind/Befuddlement is one identity. Implement the preferred 5.1 Feeblemind profile, not two roots. Five selected roots are new in 5.2: Charm Monster, Aura of Life, Vitriolic Sphere, Summon Dragon and Power Word Heal.
- Reuse native ECS entities, conditions, actions, areas, items and summon lifecycle. New concrete content and narrow hooks at existing owners are permitted in the proposed design. No generic spell interpreter, new controller system, global effect manager or attack scheduler.
- **No NDClient work**, artwork, UI, direct player possession of summons, flight/hovering project, world calendar, planar world simulator or unrelated spell implementation. Native server choices/projections and necessary generated SDK contract maintenance remain engine work.
- The human forbade non-SRD **spells**; that does not establish a blanket ban on BG 3-derived **creatures or monster actions**. The old audit's phrase “SRD forms” left the exact roster unresolved. Decision 1 explicitly proposes the bodies and loadouts below; no silent roster restriction or expansion.
- Existing unrelated non-SRD roots remain untouched. Exposing legacy Black Tentacles or implementing the excluded Maze, Fire Storm, Meteor Swarm, Dominate spells, etc. is outside this plan.

Sources: [official SRD 5.1](https://media.wizards.com/2023/downloads/dnd/SRD_CC_v5.1.pdf), [official SRD 5.2](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.pdf). The repository audit is pinned to 5.2; do not silently replace it with 5.2.1. BG 3 links accompany each affected card. Wiki values are evidence, not permission to copy documented bugs or to claim unspecified behavior is verified.

Spell modules own spell rules. Proposed `dnd/monsters/elemental_summons.py`, `planar_summons.py` and `draconic_summons.py` own concrete creature content; these are **new files**, not current implementations. Low-level passive cast/body data belongs below spell and creature composition. Content installers compose these dependencies above both. No spell-school module imports a higher-level installer; no late imports, `getattr` dispatch or circular imports.

Do not infer a complete casting snapshot from `EffectOrigin`: it retains identity/lineage/levels/position, not caster UUID, selected casting source, attack bonus, DC or growth dice. Store those explicit passive fields where needed. Do not reuse `SpellGrantingItem` for thrown spell payloads: it normally rebinds casting to the current user. Retain original caster and power; rebase only delivery position and native parent events.

Suppression and retirement are different operations. While suppressed, contributions disappear as native rules require, clocks continue using native duration rules, and nothing is accidentally cured/dismissed/detonated. Any special expiration-while-suppressed behavior is a named decision below. Rejected preparations leave the prior effect/item/summon in place; exact-once consequences follow committed removal, never removal preparation.

## Spell cards: level 4

### Charm Monster

**Rule profile.** 5.2, Action, 30 feet, visible creature, Wisdom save; advantage if caster/allies are fighting it. Non-concentration, one hour/600 native rounds. Failed save applies a creature-general charm; damage from caster or allies ends it. One additional target per slot above 4. The 5.2 text imposes no extra target-to-target spacing rule.

**Implementation / placement.** `dnd/spells/enchantment.py`: `CharmMonster`, distinct `CharmMonsterEffect`. Compose the Charm Person/Animal Friendship charm mechanics, preserving source-specific prevention of attacks against the charmer and source-owned damage removal. Remove Humanoid eligibility; do not copy Charm Person's older multi-target spacing. Identity `spell.charm_monster`; condition identity below `condition.spell.charm_monster.*`. Friendly status is represented through the existing charm relationship; no new player-control/AI allegiance system.

**Setup and acceptance.** Use the existing rank-one-two combatant/Encounter setup: Humanoid, Beast, Dragon, Undead and immune creature; allied and third-party damage sources. Force saves to verify combat advantage, one recipient at slot 4 and six at slot 9, independently saved recipients, visibility/range and exact source. Caster/ally damage clears only this cast's charm; unrelated hostile damage does not. Validate long duration, no concentration replacement and repeat-cast ownership.

**Awkward interactions / references.** Charmed immunity, native Calm Emotions **clearing/prevention**, eligible Protection from Evil and Good and its source-creature filter; Greater Restoration/Power Word Heal recognition of separate charm identities. Current CalmedEffect removes/prevents control; do not resurrect a removed charm after Calm ends. Do not replace the target's controller or grant dialogue behavior. No remaining spell-specific decision beyond the common class/source policy.

### Aura of Life

**Rule profile.** 5.2, Action, concentration up to ten minutes/100 rounds, moving 30-foot emanation. Caster and allies have necrotic resistance and cannot suffer additional max-HP reduction. An ally at 0 HP starting its turn there heals 1 HP. No revival of dead entities; no restoration of preexisting lost maximum HP.

**Implementation / placement.** `dnd/spells/abjuration.py`: caster-owned `AuraOfLifeZone` using `MembershipAreaCondition`; recipient lease `AuraOfLifeProtection` adds necrotic resistance and a passive prevention fact. Query that fact at **Harm reduction admission and LifeDrainReduction creation AND add_reduction**. A condition-admission immunity by itself misses existing Life Drain increments. Keep this query in the low recipient/Health ownership layer; no Health import of a spell class. Target-turn-start healing uses the recipient's native heal path and checks alive/downed and active membership at delivery.

**Setup and acceptance.** Caster, allied downed creature at 25 feet, enemy downed creature, dead ally and ally outside 30 feet; move caster across the boundary. Show 1 HP exactly at eligible turn start, no repeated heal at another actor's start and no dead-body healing. Apply Harm and Life Drain before entry, while inside and after exit: existing loss persists, further increments are blocked inside, normal reductions resume outside. Use an existing Life Drain owner to prove mutation prevention.

**Awkward interactions / references.** Spirit Guardians moving leases, Protection from Poison affinity, Harm and Wight Life Drain, Aid, No Healing/Chill Touch, Antimagic clipping and concentration loss. Resistance does not stack into immunity. Decision 16 resolves the source's “an ally” wording for a downed caster and ordinary countdown semantics.

### Phantasmal Killer

**Rule profile.** [BG 3](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Phantasmal_Killer): Action, 60 feet, initial Wisdom save, concentration ten turns; immobilizing fear-style condition, attack/check disadvantage and 4d10 psychic per turn, +1d10 per higher slot. The wiki establishes per-turn damage but not every save/damage boundary: **Decision 4** proposes an explicit 5.1-derived recovery order, not a claimed verified BG 3 sequence.

**Implementation / placement.** `dnd/spells/illusion.py`: own `PhantasmalKillerEffect`, retained caster/DC/slot/origin, independent control and turn handler. Reuse Dissonant Fear's movement denial/attack-check penalties and Phantasmal Force's retained-source periodic delivery, without its adaptive damage-type behavior. Native Frightened immunity/cure aliases must recognize this distinct spell condition. Never install generic Frightened plus another unowned permanent immobilizer.

**Setup and acceptance.** Caster and two nonimmune targets plus fear-immune target. Force initial success/failure, later fail then success; drive actual target turn boundaries. Assert the agreed 4d10/5d10 timing once per boundary and no tick after cure/concentration ending. Caster ability/DC changes after casting do not change stored power. Test caster death, overlapping casters and duplicate event delivery.

**Awkward interactions / references.** Heroism prevention/clearing, Calm Emotions, eligible Protection from Evil and Good, Mind-affecting save context, temporary Antimagic suppression, damage affinity and concentration checks caused by psychic damage. Do not copy BG 3 immunity/save-bonus bugs. Its immobilization is an explicit BG 3 adaptation; Weird below uses ordinary 5.1 Frightened instead.

### Resilient Sphere

**Rule profile.** [BG 3](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Otiluke%27s_Resilient_Sphere): Action, 30 feet, concentration **three turns**, Dexterity save for enemies, willing allies bypass save, no Huge-or-larger target. Half movement, invulnerability, **occupant cannot cast spells or deal damage**, and a spell/interaction boundary. **Decision 3** defines incoming healing, self effects, ongoing effects and item/action channels; the source does not fully settle them.

**Implementation / placement.** `dnd/spells/evocation.py`: `ResilientSphereEffect` on one creature, no physical pushable sphere entity. Compose source-owned speed modifier, native invulnerability at damage admission, and explicit source/recipient effect and action predicates at existing admission owners. Sanctuary and the current spell protection checks are references, not interchangeable rule profiles. Invulnerability cannot be implemented as resistance. Local condition behavior must catch natural attacks, spell attacks, breath/save effects, non-damage hostile conditions and hybrid packets; do not create an Antimagic area around the creature.

**Setup and acceptance.** Friendly and hostile targets, Huge target and normal target. Show ally no save, hostile save success/failure, legal half-speed movement, and expiration on exactly the third turn. Run the Decision 3 matrix through real actions: weapon, spell attack, save burst, breath, ongoing acid, hostile control, incoming healing, self heal and consumable use. Verify no resource costs on a rejected outward action and correct paid attempted effects at the existing outer cast boundary.

**Awkward interactions / references.** Death Ward, Power Word Kill, existing DoT, concentration maintained by occupant, forced movement, Antimagic, Dispel Magic and Disintegrate's force-creation interaction. The BG 3 condition model does not automatically inherit a wall's destruction behavior; Decision 3 states its chosen destruction policy explicitly. No wholesale Globe/Antimagic rewrite.

### Vitriolic Sphere

**Rule profile.** 5.2, Action, 150 feet, 20-foot-radius sphere, Dexterity save. Failure: 10d4 acid now and 5d4 at the end of the target's next turn. Success: half initial damage only. Higher slots add **2d4 to initial damage only** per slot above 4. Instantaneous; delayed residue is not concentration.

**Implementation / placement.** `dnd/spells/evocation.py`: root burst using Fireball's native AoE/save delivery and own `VitriolicResidue` using Acid Arrow's retained-source delayed delivery. Each failed recipient owns a separate 5d4 packet and exact deadline. A later successful cast does not clear someone else's existing residue. No invented persistent acid surface.

**Setup and acceptance.** Three creatures in the burst with failed/successful/immune saves and one outside. Fix rolls: assert 10d4 versus half, then exactly 5d4 only on failed recipients at their own next turn end. Upcast at 5 and 9 verifies 12d4/20d4 initial and unchanged 5d4 later. Affinities are evaluated separately on both deliveries, including a resistance gained in between.

**Awkward interactions / references.** Acid Arrow delayed packets, Evasion save behavior, Sphere/Globe protection, Antimagic suppression, death, caster absence, concurrent residue from two casters and secondary concentration checks. The timed packet's original DC/source cannot be rebound to the current recipient or caster stats. No remaining source-profile doubt.

## Spell cards: level 5

### Antilife Shell

**Rule profile.** 5.1, Action, self, moving ten-foot-radius shell, concentration up to one hour/600 rounds. Non-Construct/non-Undead creatures cannot pass or reach through it; spells and ranged/reach weapon attacks can pass. If the caster's movement would force an affected creature through the shell, the spell ends.

**Implementation / placement.** `dnd/spells/abjuration.py`: moving boundary owner with requester-aware crossing predicate, composed from Wind Wall crossing checks and moving aura geometry. It is not a solid opaque tile ring and does not grant allied immunity. Inspect **each committed caster movement leg/sweep**, including forced movement, not merely old/new recipient membership; crossing can occur with unchanged final membership. No new pathfinder. Decision 9 states initial occupants, movement-through-occupants and magical travel semantics.

**Setup and acceptance.** Caster, living ally, living enemy, Undead and Construct on opposite sides. Drive normal and forced movement into/out of shell; excluded creature types cross normally. Test ranged and reach attacks/casts across it. Move or push caster past a living creature, including a route whose final membership is unchanged: shell ends on the first qualifying sweep. Test canceled movement and suppressed boundary.

**Awkward interactions / references.** Wind Wall's channel-specific crossing, Spirit Guardians relocation, Thunderwave/Shove, Misty Step/Dimension Door, Forcecage, summons of each creature type and overlapping shells. Do not generalize a creature crossing block into blocking arrows, light or all teleportation.

Slot 5 has four elemental choices; slot 6+ has four myrmidon choices with no further numeric slot scaling. Decision 1 proposes all eight bodies below rather than implying they already exist.

**Implementation / placement.** `dnd/spells/summoning.py`: family root and form facet; `dnd/summoning/forms.py` authored eligibility/recipe IDs; new `dnd/monsters/elemental_summons.py` for body/action composition. Extend the existing prepared independent-owner path for caster-owned family replacement and an explicit `UNTIL_LONG_REST` lifetime. `sustain=NONE` plus arbitrary duration_rounds does not implement either requirement. Preserve old summon on rejected form/placement/birth/owner admission; retire old one only when new birth can commit.

**Creature work.** The appendix specifies HP, AC, scores, movement, affinities, action packets, local statuses/surfaces, recharge, AI and tests for **four elementals plus four myrmidons**. BG 3 body actions are native monster actions, not extra public spell roots. Do not import non-SRD spells to fill a loadout. Do not accidentally add SRD Air Form/Whelm/Earth Glide to BG 3 bodies.

**Setup and acceptance.** Use the real Game/Encounter/SummoningSystem fixture in `test_bg3_spell_summons.py`, not a factory call alone. Cast every form, advance autonomous allied turns, test a meaningful attack and each special action. Slot 5 rejects myrmidon; slot 6 exposes correct choices; slot 9 has no unintended buff. Recast succeeds once and rejected recast keeps old body. Long rest, defeat, caster death, independent-encounter transfer and Antimagic follow explicitly tested native policy.

**Awkward interactions / references.** Conjure Animals lifecycle, Spiritual Weapon independent owner, Flaming Sphere fields, elemental surface contacts, Silence/visibility/travel restrictions and buff/cure eligibility of Elementals. Form combat techniques add real content work; cast-shell success alone is insufficient.

### Dispel Evil and Good

**Rule profile.** [BG 3](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Dispel_Evil_and_Good): Action, self, concentration ten turns; attacks by Aberrations, Celestials, Elementals, Fey, Fiends and Undead have disadvantage against caster. Granted touch Action Break Enchantment removes eligible source-owned charm/fear. No tabletop Dismissal/planar return branch. Decision 17 recommends that successful Break Enchantment ends the sustaining spell, using 5.1 where BG 3 documentation is incomplete.

**Implementation / placement.** `dnd/spells/abjuration.py`: own protection condition and granted `BreakEnchantment` action through `ConcentrationActionMarker`. Reuse Protection from Evil and Good source-creature eligibility and prepared control-removal batches. Six creature types are the selected BG 3 profile, not an accidental expansion of 5.1's five. The Break Enchantment wiki documents its literal-Frightened-only behavior as a bug: use semantic aliases for separate spell conditions. No possession owner exists; do not invent a possession system or advertise curing an unrepresented state.

**Setup and acceptance.** Each listed creature type plus Humanoid attacking caster; normal attack/save distinctions. Apply charm/fear from eligible and ineligible sources, including Dissonant Whispers, Fear and Phantasmal Killer. Break only eligible effects at touch range, preserve other effects, spend Action once, and test owner ending according to Decision 17. A rejected removal must not leave a half-cleared control batch.

**Awkward interactions / references.** Protection from Evil and Good, Calm Emotions, Heroism, Power Word Heal, source defeat/unavailable source metadata, hybrid weapon spells and Antimagic suppression. Store enough source-creature provenance at admission to avoid making old effects incurable when their source dies.

### Summon Dragon

**Rule profile.** 5.2, Action, visible unoccupied space within 60 feet, concentration one hour/600 rounds. Draconic Spirit uses slot-scaled statistics and retained caster accuracy/DC. The caster separately chooses shared resistance and breath damage from Acid/Cold/Fire/Lightning/Poison. The body resists **all five**, not only the chosen breath element. Multiattack is floor(slot/2) Rends **AND** Breath Weapon.

**Implementation / placement.** `dnd/spells/summoning.py`, authored forms and proposed `dnd/monsters/draconic_summons.py`. Extend passive `SpellSummonParameters` only with concrete missing casting/choice data. Shared resistance is a caster-owned lease linked to this spell, cleaned on dismissal, replacement, defeat and concentration end; no lingering buff after body loss. Use a typed breath/resistance selection, not a misleading existing facet named `side` or a color-dependent invented damage table.

**Creature work.** Use existing `MultiattackAction` for the slot-scaled Rends. A body-owned cadence condition/handler grants **one free Breath action after the first paid Multiattack of that dragon turn**. That Breath is an ordinary native cone/save action with no additional Action/Bonus Action/Reaction cost and a consumed-once allowance. The normal controller finishes the Rends before selecting the granted Breath and aims it separately. Keep separate ordinary attack and save profiles; no mixed-outcome API, composite action or AI evaluator expansion. Native Multiattack uses equipped slots: author exact bound intrinsic Rend weapon data for its slot, retained accuracy, 10ft reach and damage; merely adding a NaturalAttack action to the creature does not wire it into Multiattack. This is native creature data, not a lootable item. Their action provenance remains separate from the creation spell. Decision 2 records the human's approved sequence and the remaining initiative/choice details.

**Setup and acceptance.** Slot 5/6/7/8/9 bodies, all five breath types and all five shared-resistance choices, including different choices on one cast. Assert exact HP/AC and 2/3/3/4/4 Rends for one Action, followed by one granted Breath for zero further action-economy cost. Primary Rend target dying does not erase the allowance. Reject free Breath before the paid Multiattack and after consumption/own turn end; repeated child attacks or a second Multiattack that turn cannot issue another allowance. Test normal native AI discovering and using the free action, ally collateral, DC/accuracy retention, summon death/concentration cleanup and every resistance.

**Awkward interactions / references.** NaturalAttack, native cone damage/save events, Flaming Sphere retained DC, existing Fly movement, Wet/Ice/Fire exposure, creature-type wards and antimagic. Autonomous allied AI is the prior approved engine model; it differs from the SRD's default-Dodge-unless-commanded clause and must be described as that adaptation.

### Flesh to Stone

**Rule profile.** [BG 3](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Flesh_to_Stone): Action, 60 feet, Constitution save, concentration; **four total failed saves**, with restrained stages after initial failure and subsequent failures, then Petrified. Do not substitute the tabletop three-failure/three-success counter. Decision 5 resolves repeat-success behavior and permanent completion versus full-duration maintenance; BG 3's documented permanence conflicts with its tooltip and is listed as a bug.

**Implementation / placement.** `dnd/spells/transmutation.py`: own staged condition retains DC and consecutive stage state. Compose Restrained penalties with a stage field, not three independent lingering conditions. On agreed final success of the transformation, apply a source-tagged native Petrified lease and detach or sustain it according to Decision 5. Concentration loss before completion clears stages.

**Native Petrified qualification.** Existing `Petrified` applies control/all-type resistance and Poisoned-condition immunity. It does **not** fully implement its description's poison-damage/disease immunity. Add the missing poison damage immunity at that native condition owner if required by the agreed transform; do not claim a nonexistent disease simulator. Retain cure tags so Greater Restoration and suppression target the correct spell-owned transform.

**Setup and acceptance.** Initial success; initial fail plus three subsequent fails; success at each intermediate stage; concentration ending at each stage and after completion. ## Summoned creatures: detailed proposed implementation

This is part of the spell implementation, not a later cosmetic task. The proposed roster contains **twelve body recipes**: eight Conjure Elemental forms, three Planar Ally forms, one scaled Draconic Spirit. Dragon damage/resistance choices are passive parameters, not twenty-five duplicated creature classes. **Decision 1** covers the BG 3 body/loadout proposal, including documented gaps and adaptations. It does not authorize non-SRD spells.

### Shared creature construction and lifecycle

- Compose each `EntityConfig` from ordinary `HealthConfig`, `EquipmentConfig`, ability scores, creature type, size, senses, native action economy and concrete actions/conditions. Use fixed max-HP composition as in the existing spell-summon bodies, not a temporary-HP overlay. AC is the authored final body AC; do not add DEX twice. XP/progression rewards for spell-created bodies follow existing summon policy.
- Form recipes have stable content IDs, action/condition dependencies, faithful provenance and explicit adaptation notes. BG 3-derived creature recipes are not mislabeled as exact SRD stat blocks. `SummonForm.minimum_slot`/family eligibility is authoritative in discovery and execution. Existing form selection is cumulative; if BG 3 higher slots replace rather than merely unlock choices, use a local allowed-form filter instead of changing every summon family.
- Ordinary movement is WALKING. Enable FLYING only through supported native grants/actions; use its normal map obstruction, landing and cost rules. The BG 3 Fly action's eighteen-meter target range is not proof of a permanent sixty-foot free-hover speed. Decision 1 proposes walk 30/fly 60 for the forms explicitly granted Fly, as a labeled native movement adaptation. Other forms retain walk 30; Water's existing swimming eligibility may be authored, but no generic swimming-speed overhaul. Draconic Spirit has exact swim 30/walk 30 and fly 60.
- No direct player-control promise: bodies join the caster's faction/party relationship and take native autonomous AI turns. Their actions, outcomes, target eligibility, movement and recharge must be visible to native AI. A body that only attacks when a test directly calls a private method has not passed.
- Base BG 3 bodies use PB 4; their power is their own authored stats, **not caster DC**. Only Draconic Spirit inherits caster PB/attack/DC. Where a technique publishes a fixed save DC, retain that value unless a source identifies it as a bug; otherwise use the explicit authored body source shown below. Do not silently apply caster DC to every summon.
- Bound armaments are summon-owned equipment/attack data and retire with the body; no farmable +3 flails, maces or scimitars. Prefer real native equipped weapon data for armed forms so mainhand attacks and opportunity reactions work, with an explicit cannot-disarm fact if the current action supports disarm. Mixed elemental rider packets remain native typed damage. For unarmed forms, explicitly configure the existing opportunity reaction to use their designated native basic strike; current generic opportunity reaction assumes `MELEE_MAIN`, so an unequipped NaturalAttack alone does not establish that feature. Qualify this narrow reaction routing rather than pretending it already works.
- Rest-sustained families need explicit `UNTIL_LONG_REST` representation in summon rules/owner composition; the current `duration_rounds:int` is insufficient. Extend the existing independent caster owner, one condition per family, to prepare replacement and retirement atomically. Conjure Elemental and Planar Ally coexist; a same-family recast replaces; different casters remain independent.
- Proposed native cleanup policy for discussion in Decision 1: caster defeat/death or explicit dismissal retires independent family bodies, as the prior approved spell-summon model does; losing ordinary concentration does not. Dragon ends on concentration/body defeat. Long rest retires the two BG 3 families and their inventory/areas; a short rest refreshes only authored short-rest uses. Every own area/local control lease has a documented lifetime on source retirement; do not leave detached fields accidentally.
- Antimagic uses existing summon suppression/contribution rules. Test that suppressed body/action/fields and shared caster resistance agree; no duplicate birth on resumption and no accidental concentration/recast replacement. Encounter transfer uses native immediate-summoner membership requirements, not a new nested rebinding system.
