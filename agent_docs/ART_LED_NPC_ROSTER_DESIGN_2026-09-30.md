# Art-led NPC roster: design and evidence

Date: 30 September 2026; revised 1 October. Status: fantasy roster curation,
Devil and stronger HD Enemy proposals integrated into the review gallery;
balance remains provisional.
Earlier approvals apply
to earlier snapshots and do not approve the current visual matches or balance.
This is a content-design deliverable. It authorizes no production
imports, native roster changes, schema changes or implementation.

## Corrected selection priority

The human correction requires a full **SRD 5.1 first** match for every artwork
variant. Search the complete creature catalogue, regardless of whether its
factory already exists locally. SRD 5.2 may supply additional matches, with
each selected profile explicitly adapted to 5.1 rules. Local implementation
status is a separate readiness fact, never the candidate-search boundary.
The earlier custom proposals in the historical reports are superseded where the revised SRD
matching selects a fitting source profile. Later direct sprite/attack inspection
can also supersede earlier visual claims. Keep flavour names and actual visible gear, and distinguish an unchanged
profile, a cosmetic reskin, a loadout change and a mechanical adaptation.

Revision plan: verify the official full sources, rematch all 259 rows, reconcile
selected rules and local gaps, then run fresh anti-slop and anti-OOP/ECS reviews.
The September 30 source/coverage checks are a historical checkpoint; the human's
subsequent visual corrections reopen affected mappings. The current bounded
revision replaces the Demon pack with the human's chosen Devil faction, excludes
30 recognizable modern zombie designs, reconciles the gallery and counts,
adds level 1–12 team coverage, and replaces the HD Enemy pack's weak
Guard/Scout/Thug proposals with stronger 5.1 foundations. Anti-slop and
anti-OOP/ECS self-reviews accompany the new Devil and HD Enemy drafts and
do not constitute fresh independent approvals. No
implementation or production import work is added.

## Intended result and source fidelity

Each owned fixed sprite variant gets one traceable proposal based on the full
SRD catalogue, with flavour name, visual evidence, selected creature, printed
source page, explicit gear/body changes, retained signatures and local gaps.
A selected block supplies all source stats, defenses, senses, movement, traits,
actions and spellcasting except for the changes expressly named in its row.
A missing animation is a presentation gap, not permission to delete an SRD
attack. Source CR is a benchmark; edited loadouts are not certified at that CR.
No encounter-strength cap was requested: Lich and other high-CR selections
retain their source strength rather than being silently weakened.

The tables distinguish exact matches, cosmetic reskins, changed loadouts,
explicit species/body/mechanical adaptations and no suitable profile. An
adaptation is not counted as an unchanged match. No-fit rows name rejected
analogues and bounded custom candidates rather than claiming final stat blocks.
Colours, uniforms, vendor labels and extra poses do not grant powers.

An art row is not a new mechanical content identity. Palette siblings can share
one composition. The fixed roster complements modular humanoids and modular
`NakedBody2` skeletons; it does not replace them or require entity classes.

## Visible equipment and 5.1 rule conversions

An SRD block is the rules foundation; visible gear determines loadout changes.
Remove incompatible stock possessions/actions and author actual replacement
weapons, shields and armor. Preserve full source traits and compute attacks
from the chosen weapon, ability and proficiency. A bow-only Goblin does not
inherit unseen blades; a sword/shield sprite receives real items. The Gladiator
with the drawn heavy hammer uses the SRD Maul even though it is absent locally;
Brute adds one extra d6 to its ordinary2d6, giving3d6+4. Ordinary unarmed
humanoids use the 5.1 unarmed baseline. The two adapted Enemy fighters explicitly
own their stronger trained Fist and Martial Guard rules; their ordinary gloves
do not grant those rules or retain an unseen Mace. Intrinsic claws, teeth,
tails and hooves remain
body actions, not loot.

Pixels sometimes resolve broad gear class without subtype. Rows state the
conservative authored item choice separately from that observation. AC comes
from the actual worn armor formula and shield; natural armor does not stack
with another AC formula. Required proficiency in replacement gear is an explicit
part of the authored loadout. Spell components and hand requirements retain
5.1 rules; empty hands do not establish component-free casting.

Five armored dinosaur proposals use actual `armor.half_plate` barding; three
fixed Character humanoids also use real half plate. Rows specify chosen AC.
Baked armor pixels remain; suppress only a duplicate overlay, retaining actual
possession. Runtime size/wearability, equipment bindings and presentation are
unverified despite the concrete design values.

The retained firearm loadouts use a converted 5.2 Musket: 1d12 piercing, range
40/120, ammunition (bullets), Loading and Two-Handed. Use 5.1 ranged attack and
ammunition rules; omit 5.2 Slow mastery. Loading permits **one shot per action**,
even if a foundation has Multiattack. The two HD Enemy gunners now use full
Assassin signatures and supplied poison ammunition, not Scout HP16. Art does
not establish automatic fire,
optics bonuses or longbow range. Local Musket/proficiency/ammunition support is
missing; the environmental arcane machine gun does not supply carried gun rules.

The secondary beasts have explicit conversions: Ankylosaurus-based Tail uses
a 5.1 Strength save for Prone; Allosaurus-based Claws/Pounce uses an approach,
save and bonus-action Bite sequence. Hippopotamus retains ordinary Bite,
two-attack Multiattack and Hold Breath in 5.1 representation. Ogre Zombies keep
**5.1** defenses/Fortitude while replacing unseen Morningstars with Slam; they
do not acquire 5.2 exhaustion immunity. The companion rows control exact values.

Optional Toxic Spit remains a separate shared content candidate, outside the
selected Allosaurus block and unproved by a Spit clip. `NaturalAttack` accepts
ranged metadata but uses a default `MELEE_MAIN` proxy in outcomes/events.
Discovery, ranged bonuses, delivery and slot-sensitive behavior are unverified.

## Player levels 1–12 and teams

The human requires enemy coverage across player levels **1 through 12**, with
substantial opponents and teams whose abilities cooperate. The Devil draft
provides one exact-key team for each of the twelve levels, using four
adventurers as a stated planning assumption. Its owned profiles span provisional
CR1/8–12, including six distinct officer/boss designs at target CR5–12, flyers,
armored frontliners, marksmen and staff controllers. It is not merely a set of
weak source blocks with elite names. CR targets and difficulty remain provisional
until balance and encounter playback are tested.

Teams include shield screens with ranged pressure, Grease with close melee,
Web with archers/blades, Bless with durable frontliners, glaive reach with a
close fighter and archer, and a proper legendary boss with a separate support
caster. Costs and counterplay are explicit: one concentration spell per caster,
ordinary Help scope, actual reaction timing, and no free ally turns or taunts.
Prone does not improve distant bow attacks, Bane does not reduce grapple ability
checks, and magical-darkness teams include only creatures explicitly equipped
with Devil's Sight. The 12-row table contains conditional 2014 encounter XP
arithmetic; it does not import 5.2's different budgets.

Use the remaining SRD-matched factions as alternative teams at their genuine
strengths. Do not weaken a genuine Lich to fill a mid-tier hole, turn optional
zombie backstories into mechanics, or count excluded artwork as progression
coverage. The stronger HD Enemy draft adds eight squads for levels5–12: shield
troops and Leadership, contested Shove with nearby Maul attacks, caster screens,
eligible Sneak Attack positioning, and an Archmage boss with battlefield
partitioning. Friendly Burst cones, poison defenses, ranged disadvantage against
prone enemies, spell concentration and actual Wall of Force obstruction are
included. Source-CR XP estimates remain conditional on the edited profiles.
The [review gallery](../.runtime/pack-study-20260930/srd-first/illustration-comparison/index.html)
exposes the level/team plans alongside creature comparisons, kit notes and
match certainty.

## Native composition and readiness

The [native audit](audits/PACK_ROSTER_NATIVE_COMPOSITION_2026-09-30.md) locates
existing owners. Reuse native data, builders and systems:

- `dnd/monsters/bestiary.py`, `bestiary_content.py`, and `srd_roster.py` own
  existing creature facts/compositions; configured presentation variants do
  not establish additional full SRD implementations.
- `dnd/monsters/traits.py`, skeleton abilities and `dnd/body_responses.py` own
  natural attacks, traits, riders, reactions and material responses.
- `dnd/content/items/` owns actual equipment and loadouts.
- `dnd/content/characters/builds.py` owns player class/progression builds when
  those are actually intended. A fixed sprite does not require a PC class.

No NPC class hierarchy, manager or parallel roster framework is proposed.
Registration is not full parity: for example, local Dretch HP, actions and
signature support differ from the complete source. Missing Worg, Quasit, Imp,
Hezrou, Vrock, Wight, Lich, Shadow, Mastiff and other selected profiles remain
honest content/support gaps. Full prepared spell lists replace the earlier
arbitrary caster shortlists without claiming implemented spell parity.

Elite palettes alone keep the selected rules. Species/body adaptations state
the retained source size/type/actions and their mismatch with artwork; no
unseen anatomical feature is asserted as a visual finding. Grounded humanoid
source profiles in the Demons pack explicitly retain Humanoid type. A new
signature does not follow from a pack label or effect colour.

## Presentation and retained-history boundary

Native composition owns stable game identity, appearance facts, actual
possessions and actions. Subjective capture admits only permitted historical
identity, appearance, visible kit and action results. Presentation uses those
retained facts to choose a fitting registered rig and existing authored recipe.
Neither native construction nor action execution reads sprites, media paths,
contact frames, source filenames, installed-art readiness or renderer output.

`game/combat.py:actor_contact` already resolves creature identities through
`AnimationData.creature_rigs`; `resolve_actor_layers` already separates modular
equipment from fixed body/shadow layers. Retain this direction. Existing legacy
presentation metadata in native descriptors is acknowledged debt, not a reason
to expand its coupling or start a global migration in this study.

Every action remains part of its complete actual causal lineage. Playback uses
the admitted history, its preceding actor state and exact results. Later native
state and later subjective reduction cannot change an older pose, loadout,
contact, HP or endpoint. Do not query live Entity/GridMap data during replay,
flatten reactions into attack effects, or add a parallel render-event taxonomy.

Unsupported disarm, dropped-weapon, alternate-loadout or cast poses are honest
presentation gaps. The native equipment/action fact remains valid. Before any
fixed content is exposed as visually complete, either select existing matching
art/modular presentation for those states or name the limitation; leaving a
baked weapon visible does not prove correct equipment playback.

Goblin11/12 are fixed mounted-rider pictures. Their proposals preserve distinct
rider and steed identities, gear and natural attacks; no native riding composer
was found. The baked composite also cannot presently portray independently
moving, targeted, dismounted or dead participants. This is a named future
capability question for those entries, not permission to manufacture a single
larger Goblin or a renderer-only mount relationship. It does not block the rest
of the roster study.

## Shared rig and action adaptation

Reuse `BodyRig`, `BodyClip`, pose sockets, `AttackVariant`, Studio cast drafts
and existing body-action recipes. Their explicit sheets, frame counts, facing
rows, FPS and support/rest anchors already describe fixed source layout.
`source_clip` is provenance/mapping, not a rule selector.

Source `Attack 3` on Goblin01 depicts airborne axe melee; Goblin03's Attack1/2
depict bow use; SkeletonArcher05's semantic Attack3 maps to QuickShot. These are
different source meanings. The current attack selector matches retained item,
slot, damage and outcome facts but has no rig-specific profile choice. A renamed
clip cannot repair a different contact/release frame or baked effect track.

The preferred direction is a small presentation-owned association from the
selected rig and existing recipe/profile identity to a compatible authored
body/profile override, using the current typed records. Shared resolution must
cover attack, cast and actor-only action consumers. This is a design candidate,
not a prescribed schema edit. The completed timing audit supports the association
and leaves its exact record placement to a later implementation plan. Root
modular recipes, preparation/body joins, actual release/contact,
recovery and elapsed-time execution retain their current meanings.

Resolve rig adaptation after the existing semantic selector. For attacks the
selected identity includes recipe and `AttackVariant.id`; casts and actor-only
actions retain their own recipe identities. A clip-level marker alone is
insufficient when two actions share one clip but need different anchors.
Keep each record's existing anchor vocabulary and validate resolved frames
against the selected clip. Absent overrides retain valid recipe defaults;
unavailable clips or unreachable markers remain explicit support gaps. Exact
key/record placement is still a schema-design decision for a selected later
implementation, not another authoring format introduced by this study.

The current source execution settles at the last sampled frame:
`body_duration = (frames - 1) * 1000 / (fps * playback_speed)`.
An action marker occurs at `frame * 1000 / (fps * playback_speed)`.
Preserve those equations, including root modular timing; do not retime clips
to the editor's count/FPS estimate or add a one-past-end marker.

Do not force all clips to 15 frames. Orc/Goblin blocks use six, Demon blocks
seven; Character Knight/Paladin/DeathKnight casts have sixteen. `BodyClip.frames`
already varies while `BodyFrame` presently caps authored markers at fourteen.
An extension, if a selected marker needs it, should validate against its actual
clip. A sixteen-frame clip alone does not require a release marker on frame
fifteen. Source sheets supply no vendor FPS or contact markers; chosen playback
rates and semantic markers remain explicit reviewed authoring.

## Shadows and effects

Separate source body/shadow/effect tracks are preferred. Orc/Goblin, Demons and
Top-down Zombies supply such sources. Some effect exports are placeholders;
inspect actual pixels before binding. Demon attack FX should not disappear
merely because the current installation omitted them.

Animals, HD Undead, HD Enemy and the HD humanoid/zombie packs supply paired
shadowless/with-shadow alternatives. Use those pairs for deterministic offline
extraction probes; preserve the shadowless body unchanged. A mask only outside
the body cannot be assumed to reproduce semitransparent edges. The completed
shadow audit distinguishes exact visible recomposition from incomplete
hidden shadow recovery. The previous NeuroClient color/alpha extraction is a
source to inspect, not a license to key arbitrary dark body colours away.

Keep ground shadow registration and drawing separate from airborne body motion
and attack FX. No default second shadow goes over an already combined sheet.
Original art stays private and preserved; probes do not install production art.

The [focused shadow audit](audits/PACK_ROSTER_SHADOW_TIMING_2026-09-30.md)
recovers source RGBA shadows for GreyWolf Idle/Run/Attack1/Die and fixed
SkeletonArcher05 Idle/Run/QuickShot/Die. Each recomposed atlas matches its
with-shadow source exactly across fifteen frames and eight facings. The tested
body pixels are opaque; the exterior shadow keeps its original varying alpha.
This proves those eight selected pairs, not all clips in either pack. The
recalled NeuroClient color/alpha extractor remains unidentified after a source
and history search; that is not evidence it never existed.
